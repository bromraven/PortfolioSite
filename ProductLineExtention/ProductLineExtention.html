Endura blade product line extended to a DSLM camera

Nov 4 - Dec 16 2025

Distilled asthetics elements from source product, preformed erganomic study for feature placement.

The Tajima Endura Blade is a box cutter refill package. I extended its product asthetics out into a DSLM (Digital Single Lens Mirrorless) camera. I chose a camera becuase it can easily utilize every surface and asthetic feature of the original product, except translated to diferent fatures and activities.

This chair project was made of a single sheet of birch plywood with a CNC Router. I wanted a recliner that was relatively comfortable in both prone and relaxed positions, While being as simple and easy to construct as possible. I used the airline seat dimensions from the 1966 study Measure of Man ergonomic study.
