Product Line Extention

Endura blade product line extended to a DSLM camera

Nov 4 - Dec 16 2025

Distilled aesthetic elements from source product, performed ergonomic study for feature placement.

The Tajima Endura Blade is a box cutter refill package. I extended its product aesthetics out into a DSLM (Digital Single Lens Mirrorless) camera. I chose a camera because it can easily utilize every surface and aesthetic feature of the original product, except translated to different features and activities.

User testing conducted to determine most accessible design and feature placement. Interviewed people with large and hand sizes as well as a wide variety of familiarity with photography and cameras. This informed the logos and placement of sliders controlling aperture, exposure time, and f stop etc.
